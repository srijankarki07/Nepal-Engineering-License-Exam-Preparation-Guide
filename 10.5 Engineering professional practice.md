## **10.5 Engineering Professional Practice**

### **1. Environment and Society**

#### **Concept**

Engineering projects affect both **natural** and **social** environments positively (development, employment) and negatively (pollution, displacement).

#### **Key Points**

- Engineers must balance **economic development** with **environmental protection**.
- Must follow **Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)** and **Initial Environmental Examination (IEE)** as per Nepal’s **Environment Protection Act**.
- Sustainable development: meeting present needs **without compromising** future generations’ ability to meet theirs.

> “Which process ensures environmental compatibility of a project?”<br> > **Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)**

---

### **2. Professional Ethics**

#### **Definition**

Professional ethics = moral principles guiding behavior of engineers.

### **Core Principles (from Engineering Code of Ethics):**

1. **Integrity** – Be honest and fair.
2. **Competence** – Perform services only in your area of expertise.
3. **Public Safety** – Hold paramount the safety, health, and welfare of the public.
4. **Confidentiality** – Respect client and company information.
5. **Accountability** – Accept responsibility for one’s actions.
6. **Sustainability** – Use resources efficiently and minimize environmental harm.

**Example:**
If an engineer discovers a safety flaw in design, they must report it. **Public safety comes first** even if it affects the company.

---

### **3. Regulatory Environment**

#### **Concept**

Engineers in Nepal operate under several laws and institutions regulating their professional practice.

**Main Regulations:**

- **Nepal Engineering Council (NEC) Act 2055 (1998)** – Licensing and registration of engineers.
- **Public Procurement Act & Regulation** – Governs tenders, contracts.
- **Environmental Protection Act** – For EIA/IEE compliance.

**Purpose:**
To ensure that engineering practices are **safe, legal, and ethical**.

_Which act governs the registration of engineers in Nepal?_<br>
**Answer: Nepal Engineering Council Act 2055 (1998)**

---

### **4. Contemporary Issues / Problems in Engineering**

#### **Common Current Issues:**

- Climate change and sustainable design
- Rapid urbanization and infrastructure strain
- Lack of quality control in projects
- Brain drain of engineers
- Ethical lapses in construction supervision

---

### **5. Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)**

#### **Concept**

OHS ensures workers’ safety and well-being in workplaces like construction sites, factories, etc.

#### **Key Principles:**

- Identify workplace hazards (physical, chemical, mechanical).
- Implement preventive measures (PPE, safety training, signage).
- Maintain emergency preparedness (fire safety, first aid).
- Follow safety standards like **ILO conventions** and **Labor Act of Nepal**.

---

### **6. Roles and Responsibilities of Nepal Engineers Association (NEA)**

#### **About NEA:**

- Established in **1962** (2019 B.S.)
- Non-governmental, non-profit organization representing professional engineers in Nepal.

#### **Key Roles:**

- Promote welfare and rights of engineers
- Provide training, seminars, and professional development
- Promote ethical and responsible engineering practice
- Advise government on engineering and policy issues
- Strengthen networking among engineers

> “When was NEA established?”<br> > **2019 B.S. (1962 A.D.)**
