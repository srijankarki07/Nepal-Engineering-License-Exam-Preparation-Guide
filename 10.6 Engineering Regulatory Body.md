# **10.6 Engineering Regulatory Body: Nepal Engineering Council (NEC)**

## **1. Overview and Purpose of NEC**

**Nepal Engineering Council (NEC)** is a **statutory body** established by the **Nepal Engineering Council Act, 2055 (1998 A.D.)**.

**Purpose:**
To regulate and control the **engineering profession and education** in Nepal to ensure quality, ethics, and public safety.

---

## **2. Objectives of NEC**

According to the NEC Act, its main objectives are:

- To **register professional engineers and technicians**.
- To **set minimum educational standards** for engineering institutions.
- To **regulate the practice** of engineering.
- To **maintain the dignity and code of conduct** of engineers.
- To **advise the government** on engineering-related policies.

> “Which body sets standards for engineering education and licensing in Nepal?”<br>
> → **Nepal Engineering Council**

---

## **3. Composition and Structure of NEC**

The council consists of **21 members**, including:

- **Chairperson** – appointed by the Government of Nepal.
- **Vice-chairperson** – elected by the council members.
- **Members** – representatives from universities, government, NEA, and registered engineers.
- **Registrar** – acts as the administrative head of the council.

> “Who appoints the Chairperson of NEC?” <br> > **Government of Nepal**

---

## **4. Functions, Duties, and Powers of NEC**

According to the Act, NEC has the following major responsibilities:

| Function                | Description                                             |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Registration**        | Register engineers and issue licenses.                  |
| **Accreditation**       | Approve and monitor engineering curricula and colleges. |
| **Regulation**          | Enforce code of conduct and professional discipline.    |
| **Advisory**            | Provide technical advice to the government.             |
| **Inspection**          | Inspect engineering institutions to ensure quality.     |
| **Disciplinary action** | Take action against unethical or unqualified practice.  |

---

## **5. Registration and Licensing**

### **Types of Registration under NEC**

1. **Professional Engineer (PE)** – requires experience and qualification beyond bachelor’s.
2. **Engineer** – bachelor’s degree in engineering (minimum requirement).
3. **Sub-engineer** – diploma level (3 years after SLC/SEE).
4. **Technician** – lower-level technical training.

**Licensing Requirement:**

- You **must register with NEC** to practice engineering legally in Nepal.
- Without registration, practicing or representing as an engineer is **illegal** and **punishable**.

**Renewal:**

- Registration is **valid for 5 years** and must be renewed.

---

## **6. Key Provisions and Penalties**

- Practicing without NEC registration → **punishable offence.**
- NEC has authority to **suspend or cancel** registration for:

  - Professional misconduct
  - Negligence or unethical behavior
  - False information during registration
