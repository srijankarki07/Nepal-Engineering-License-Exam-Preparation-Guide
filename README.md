# **Nepal Engineering License Exam Preparation Guide**

A comprehensive, structured, and easy-to-use guide for preparing for the **Nepal Engineering Council (NEC) Engineering License Examination**.
This repository collects **all essential revision notes, concepts, chapter-wise summaries, and subtopic explanations** as defined by the official NEC syllabus.

---

## **About This Repository**

The **Nepal Engineering License Exam Preparation Guide** is created to help engineering graduates prepare effectively for the NEC Licensing Examination.
The goal is to provide:

- Clear and concise revision materials
- Chapter-wise breakdown of all topics
- Coverage of all subtopics listed by the **Nepal Engineering Council**
- Quick reference notes to support structured exam preparation
- A free and open learning resource for all engineering students in Nepal

This guide is designed to save time and improve revision quality by centralizing all required concepts in one place.

---

## 📚 **What’s Included**

The repository includes materials organized according to the official NEC syllabus, such as:

- **Core Engineering Concepts**
- **Fundamentals and Principles**
- **Subject-wise Chapter Summaries**
- **Solved Concepts, Notes, and Key Points**
- **Important Formulae and Definitions**
- **NEC-specific Chapter and Subtopic Coverage**

Each folder corresponds to a chapter defined by NEC, and each file contains well-structured notes for easy understanding and quick revision.

---
